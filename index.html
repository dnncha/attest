Your text messages, ready for court.

Attest turns an iMessage, SMS or WhatsApp conversation into a clear PDF exhibit: every message, who sent it, and exactly when.

Conversations in other apps, such as Instagram, Messenger, Signal or Telegram, can be added from screenshots. Each screenshot is reproduced with its own fingerprint.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

Complete

Every message in the period you choose. If you export only part of a conversation, the exhibit says how many messages came before and after.

Identified

Full date, time and time zone on every message, with the sender's name and phone number.

Verifiable

Each exhibit carries a SHA-256 fingerprint and an export record that says how and when the messages were obtained.

Private

No account and no server. Your conversations stay on your iPhone until you share the file yourself.

What is in an exhibit

  • A cover page with the parties, their numbers, the period and the device
  • An index of dates, so a judge can find a day quickly
  • Numbered messages, with the exhibit label and page number on every page
  • Photos shown in place, with the original files in the evidence package
  • A declaration page for you to sign, in United States or England and Wales form
  • Optional Bates numbering for lawyers

One price, no subscription

Exhibits of up to 25 messages are free, and you see the whole exhibit before paying. Longer exhibits need a single purchase or a 7-day pass. Nothing renews by itself.

Importing from Messages

iOS lets an app read your texts only through Apple's Shortcuts app, with your permission. Attest uses a small shortcut for this.
