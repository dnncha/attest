Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Attest collects nothing. Your conversations stay on your iPhone.

What Attest stores

Conversations you import, the names and numbers you add, and your exhibit settings are stored on your device, inside the app. Deleting a conversation in Attest, or deleting the app, removes them.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you share it. Attest has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. An exported PDF or evidence package leaves your iPhone only when you send or save it with the iOS share sheet.

Messages and Shortcuts

When you import from Messages, Apple's Shortcuts app reads the conversation you choose, with your permission, and passes it to Attest on the same device. The shortcut has no network steps.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. Attest learns only whether a purchase is active. It never sees your name, email or payment details.

Other people's messages

A conversation includes messages written by other people. You are responsible for having a lawful reason to export and share them, such as a legal dispute you are part of.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
