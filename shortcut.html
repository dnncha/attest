Export to Attest

This shortcut sends one conversation from Messages to Attest on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Download shortcut

Needs iOS 26.4 or later, with Attest installed. The quickest way is inside Attest: tap Import, then Messages, then Add shortcut.

  1. Add the shortcutIn Attest, tap Import, then Messages, then Add shortcut. Choose Shortcuts in the list and tap Add Shortcut. From this page instead: tap Download shortcut, open the download in Safari, and tap Add Shortcut. You do this once.
  2. Start an importIn Attest, tap Import, then Messages, then Start import.
  3. Choose the conversation and datesA year or less at a time works best. When iOS asks whether the shortcut may read your messages, choose Allow.
  4. Wait for Attest to openLong conversations can take a few minutes. Keep your iPhone unlocked while it runs.

What the shortcut does

It asks which conversation and which dates, reads the messages Messages returns for those dates, keeps the ones from that conversation with their date, sender and service, and hands that list to Attest. It has no network steps. You can open it in the Shortcuts app and read every step.
