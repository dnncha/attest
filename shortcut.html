Export to Attest

This shortcut sends one conversation from Messages to Attest on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Add shortcut

Needs iOS 26.4 or later, with Attest installed. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.

  1. Add the shortcutTap Add shortcut above, then Add Shortcut in the Shortcuts app. You do this once.
  2. Start an importIn Attest, tap Import, then Messages, then Start import.
  3. Choose the conversation and datesWhen iOS asks whether the shortcut may read your messages, choose Allow.
  4. Wait for Attest to openLong conversations can take a few minutes. Keep your iPhone unlocked while it runs.

What the shortcut does

It asks Messages for the conversation you pick, reads each message with its date, sender and service, and hands that list to Attest. It has no network steps. You can open it in the Shortcuts app and read every step.
