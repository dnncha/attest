Importing

Why does Messages import need a shortcut?

Apple does not let apps read your texts directly. The Shortcuts app can, with your permission, so Attest uses a shortcut to receive them. See the shortcut page.

The shortcut says it cannot find the Messages actions

Reading messages in Shortcuts needs iOS 26.4 or later. Update in Settings, General, Software Update.

The import stops part way

Keep the iPhone unlocked and plugged in for very long conversations. If it still stops, import a shorter period, such as one year at a time.

My conversation is longer than a year

Run the import again for each year. Attest joins imports of the same conversation into one, without duplicates.

Do I need to buy the Shortcuts app?

No. Shortcuts is a free Apple app that is already on your iPhone.

Can I use Instagram, Messenger, Signal or Telegram?

Yes, from screenshots. Take screenshots of the conversation from top to bottom, then in Attest tap Import and Screenshots. Each image is shown in the exhibit with its fingerprint, and the text is read from it so the PDF can be searched. This also works on iPhones older than iOS 26.4.

How do I import WhatsApp?

In WhatsApp, open the chat, tap the name at the top, then Export Chat. Choose Attach Media if you want photos in the exhibit, then pick Attest in the share sheet.

A name or number is missing

Open the conversation in Attest and tap Edit beside People. WhatsApp exports only contain names, so add the phone numbers there.

Exhibits

Will a court accept my exhibit?

Attest cannot promise that. Courts set their own rules and a judge decides. Attest follows what courts usually ask for: complete conversations, a date and time on every message, identified senders, numbered pages and a signed declaration. Keep the original messages on your phone, and check your court's rules or ask a lawyer.

What is the fingerprint?

A SHA-256 hash of the exported messages. If a single character changes, the fingerprint changes, so anyone can check that the record was not altered after export.

What is in the evidence package?

A zip file with the PDF exhibit, a spreadsheet of every message, a plain text copy, the original photos and files, and a manifest that lists the fingerprint of each file.

Should I export part of a conversation?

Courts prefer the whole conversation. If you export a period, Attest includes every message in that period and states how many earlier and later messages were left out.

Purchases

Is there a subscription?

No. There is a one-time purchase, and a 7-day pass that ends by itself.

I bought Attest and it is locked on a new phone

Open Settings in Attest and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.
