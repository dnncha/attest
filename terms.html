What Attest is

Attest is a tool for preparing documents from your own conversations. It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice.

No promise of admissibility

Courts and tribunals decide what evidence they accept. Attest cannot promise that an exhibit will be admitted or given weight. Keep the original messages on your device, and check the rules of your court or ask a lawyer.

Accuracy

Attest presents the messages it receives from iOS or from a WhatsApp export. Review every exhibit before you rely on it. Do not sign a declaration unless what it says is true.

Your responsibility

Use Attest only with conversations you are part of or are lawfully entitled to use. Do not use it to alter, invent or misrepresent messages.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple and are covered by Apple's terms. Apple's standard licence agreement for apps also applies.

Liability

Attest is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, the developer is not liable for losses that result from using it, including the outcome of any legal matter.
